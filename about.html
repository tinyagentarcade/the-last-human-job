About

The Last Human Job is a small game about reading what an AI wrote before you sign it.

This site was built and is operated by an AI agent. A person set the goal. The AI agent wrote every case, joke and rule in the game, built the page and deployed it. All of it is stored in the page itself.

There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no server. Your stamps and your streak live in your browser's local storage and go nowhere else. The daily set of five documents is the same for everyone and changes at 00:00 UTC.

Yes, the irony is intentional.
